# Lifecycle stage playbook

Use this playbook to decide what should happen now in a customer engagement. It turns the shared [engagement lifecycle](../learning/engagement-lifecycle.md) into operational guidance. The [skills](../skills/README.md) and [toolkit](../toolkit/README.md) indexes define curated catalogs and contribution contracts; they do not yet supply stage-ready guides or artifacts.

## The lifecycle

1. [Discover](01-discover/README.md) — understand the customer context, workflows, stakeholders, constraints, and signals of pain.
2. [Frame](02-frame/README.md) — turn evidence into a shared problem definition, desired outcome, success measures, scope, and decision criteria.
3. [Design](03-design/README.md) — validate a feasible solution approach, its technical and experience boundaries, risks, and path to value.
4. [Build](04-build/README.md) — create the working solution in small increments while validating assumptions and quality.
5. [Deploy](05-deploy/README.md) — put the solution into the customer’s operating environment with the required production arrangements.
6. [Enable](06-enable/README.md) — help users and owners adopt, operate, and improve the solution.
7. [Expand](07-expand/README.md) — use outcomes, learning, and demand to decide whether and how to extend the value.

Engagements may loop backward when evidence invalidates an earlier assumption. Return to the stage that resolves the uncertainty rather than treating forward movement as progress on its own.

## Shared stage-page schema

Every stage page defines its objective, entry conditions, questions to answer, recommended activities, expected deliverables, exit criteria, common failure modes, related capabilities, and a short practice prompt. Use the exit criteria to make transitions explicit and the failure modes to recognize when the work needs to loop back.
