# Expand

Expand uses demonstrated value and new demand to choose the next responsible investment.

## Objective

Use outcomes, learning, and demand to decide whether and how to extend the value.

## Entry conditions

- Users and owners can adopt, operate, and improve the solution with appropriate independence.
- Sustained adoption and outcome evidence is available.
- New demand, adjacent workflows, users, or scope are being considered.

## Questions to answer

- What evidence shows that the current outcome creates sustained value?
- Which new users, workflows, or scope have a justified connection to that value?
- What assumptions, constraints, and risks change when the work expands?
- Should the next investment extend the current solution, improve the existing workflow, or begin a new discovery cycle?

## Recommended activities

- Review outcome, adoption, operational, and demand evidence with customer decision-makers.
- Compare expansion opportunities against the original success measures, capacity, constraints, and strategic priorities.
- Define the next hypothesis, scope boundary, and evidence needed to justify investment.
- Return to Discover when a proposed expansion introduces a new workflow, stakeholder group, or untested problem.

## Expected deliverables

- An evidence-backed expansion decision: extend, improve, pause, or begin a new discovery cycle.
- A prioritized opportunity map with expected outcomes, constraints, risks, and decision criteria.
- A new or updated engagement frame for the selected next investment.
- A record of what was learned from the completed lifecycle.

## Exit criteria

- The customer and delivery team have an evidence-based decision about whether and how to extend value.
- Any approved expansion has a clear next hypothesis, scope boundary, and accountable decision-makers.
- Work that introduces new uncertainty is explicitly routed back to Discover or the appropriate earlier stage.

## Common failure modes

- Expanding because interest is high without evidence that the current outcome creates value.
- Treating requests from new users or workflows as simple scale-up when they require fresh discovery.
- Allowing scope growth to outpace operating ownership, support capacity, or outcome measurement.
- Ignoring lessons from the first lifecycle and repeating the same assumptions.
