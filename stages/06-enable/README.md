# Enable

Enable turns production availability into sustained customer capability and ownership.

## Objective

Help users and owners adopt, operate, and improve the solution.

## Entry conditions

- The outcome is available for the intended people to use.
- Production operations, support, and ownership arrangements are in place.
- The team can observe use, feedback, and outcome signals after release.

## Questions to answer

- Do intended users understand when and how to use the solution in their workflow?
- Can customer owners operate, support, and improve it without relying on the delivery team?
- What adoption, quality, and outcome signals show sustained value or friction?
- Which feedback requires an operational improvement, a product change, or a return to an earlier lifecycle stage?

## Recommended activities

- Prepare role-appropriate onboarding, documentation, support routes, and operating handoffs.
- Observe adoption in the real workflow and collect feedback from users and owners.
- Coach owners through routine operation, troubleshooting, and improvement decisions.
- Review outcome signals regularly and prioritize improvements with the customer.

## Expected deliverables

- An enablement plan and materials tailored to users, operators, and accountable owners.
- Confirmed operating ownership, support routes, and improvement cadence.
- Adoption and outcome evidence, including feedback and unresolved friction.
- A prioritized improvement backlog or decision to revisit an earlier stage.

## Exit criteria

- Users and owners can adopt, operate, and improve the solution with appropriate independence.
- Adoption and outcome evidence shows whether the intended value is being sustained.
- The customer and delivery team have a shared view of the justified next investment, if any.

## Common failure modes

- Equating access to the solution with adoption of a changed workflow.
- Delivering generic training that does not address real roles, decisions, or support needs.
- Retaining delivery-team ownership after customer owners are expected to operate the outcome.
- Ignoring feedback that shows the problem frame, design, or deployment assumptions were wrong.

## Practice

Pick a released solution and define a 30-day enablement check-in. Include one user behavior to observe, one owner capability to confirm, one outcome measure, and the decision you would make if adoption is weak.
