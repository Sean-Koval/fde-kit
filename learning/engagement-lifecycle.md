# Engagement lifecycle

The FDE engagement lifecycle is:

**Discover → Frame → Design → Build → Deploy → Enable → Expand**

It is the operational spine of the repository. Each stage resolves a different uncertainty and establishes the conditions for the next. Teams may loop backward when evidence invalidates an earlier assumption.

## Discover

Understand the customer’s context, workflows, stakeholders, constraints, and signals of pain. The transition to Frame occurs when the team has enough evidence to discuss a specific problem rather than a collection of symptoms.

## Frame

Turn evidence into a shared problem definition, desired outcome, success measures, scope, and decision criteria. Move to Design when the customer and delivery team agree on the problem worth solving and how they will judge progress.

## Design

Explore and validate a feasible solution approach, including experience, technical boundaries, risks, and a path to value. Move to Build when the approach is credible enough to implement and test with real users or operations.

## Build

Create the working solution in small increments, validating assumptions and quality as the work progresses. Move to Deploy when the solution satisfies its intended use and has a realistic production path.

## Deploy

Put the solution into the customer’s operating environment with the required reliability, security, observability, and support arrangements. Move to Enable when the outcome is available for the intended people to use.

## Enable

Help users and owners adopt, operate, and improve the solution. Move to Expand when evidence shows sustained value and identifies a justified next investment.

## Expand

Use outcomes, learning, and demand to decide whether and how to extend the value. Expansion can introduce new workflows, users, or scope, and often begins another discovery cycle.

## Using this model

Learning pages provide the shared concepts behind the lifecycle. Stage pages will provide the detailed guidance for what to do now; skills, toolkit assets, and examples will support the work without duplicating it.
