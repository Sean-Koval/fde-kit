# FDE examples

Examples demonstrate what good FDE work looks like through completed outputs. They are not instructional guides or blank reusable artifacts: use [skills](../skills/README.md) to learn a capability and the [toolkit](../toolkit/README.md) to use an artifact in the field.

## Preferred format

The preferred future example is one coherent fictional engagement. It should follow a customer from initial context through a decision-ready executive update so readers can see how evidence, decisions, and outputs connect across the lifecycle.

## Future artifact sequence

| Sequence | Completed artifact | Lifecycle stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Customer context and engagement mandate | [Discover](../stages/01-discover/README.md) |
| 2 | Stakeholder map, workflow view, and discovery evidence | [Discover](../stages/01-discover/README.md) |
| 3 | Problem frame, success measures, and scope decision | [Frame](../stages/02-frame/README.md) |
| 4 | Solution design, risks, and trade-off decision | [Design](../stages/03-design/README.md) |
| 5 | Prototype scope, validation plan, and findings | [Design](../stages/03-design/README.md), [Build](../stages/04-build/README.md) |
| 6 | Increment plan, acceptance evidence, and production-readiness decisions | [Build](../stages/04-build/README.md), [Deploy](../stages/05-deploy/README.md) |
| 7 | Deployment record, operational handoff, and enablement evidence | [Deploy](../stages/05-deploy/README.md), [Enable](../stages/06-enable/README.md) |
| 8 | Outcome review, expansion decision, and executive update | [Enable](../stages/06-enable/README.md), [Expand](../stages/07-expand/README.md) |

Add examples as completed, internally consistent outputs from this fictional engagement. Do not repeat stage instructions, turn an example into a blank template, or collect unrelated samples without the customer context that makes the decisions understandable.
