# FDE examples

Examples contains completed outputs that demonstrate good FDE work. Published examples are not instructional guides or blank reusable artifacts: use [skills](../skills/README.md) to learn a capability and the [toolkit](../toolkit/README.md) to use a reusable artifact in the field.

## Published example

### [AI-assisted invoice intake](invoice-intake-ai/README.md)

This fictional accounts-payable engagement follows one bounded workflow through all seven lifecycle stages. It connects operational evidence to a target, human/software/AI architecture, evaluation thresholds, staged rollout, Day 2 ownership, measured results, limitations, and explicit expand/hold/stop decisions.

| What to inspect | Completed content |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence and framing | Baseline workflow trace, opportunity comparison, scope, target, and counterfactual |
| Controlled AI system | Architecture and responsibility boundaries for AI, deterministic software, and accountable humans |
| Release proof | Versioned evaluation results, thresholds, staged gates, rollback rehearsal, and limitations |
| Durable outcome | Day 2 owners, adoption and operating signals, measured business result, and expansion reasoning |
| Toolkit excerpts | The seven original [field artifacts](../toolkit/README.md) (opportunity scorecard through business case), completed for one coherent engagement |

## Contribution contract

Add examples as coherent, completed engagements with enough context to make their evidence and decisions understandable. Label fictional material, keep figures internally consistent, show completed artifact excerpts rather than repeating template instructions, and make limitations visible. Do not turn an example into a blank template or collect unrelated outputs without an engagement narrative.
